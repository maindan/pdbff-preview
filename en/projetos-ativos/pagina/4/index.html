MOORE2022 Since 2023

BDFFP Forest Plots Monitoring Program for the Census Cycle 2022-2025

This project intends to conduct a new census of 69 1ha plots of the BDFFP that have been monitored since the early 1980s; continue the taxonomic identification and revision of the sampled trees; produce tools for easier plant identification in the Amazon; and finalize the data repository to make BDFFP tree data more visible, accessible, and useable.

Science for Amazon conservation

Since 1979, we have studied how fragmentation of the Amazon rainforest affects biodiversity. We turn science into conservation through long-term research, training new scientists and a continuous presence in the region.
