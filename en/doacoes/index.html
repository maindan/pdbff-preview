Support BDFFP

Why donate?

BDFFP depends on continued support to maintain rare and essential research infrastructure for understanding the Amazon rainforest.

  • Fund research on biodiversity and forest fragmentation.

  • Support field logistics, monitoring, and maintenance of study areas.

  • Train students, early-career researchers, and local field teams.

Tax-deductible donations for U.S. citizens

U.S. citizens can make tax-deductible donations to BDFFP through the Smithsonian Tropical Research Institute (STRI). Please include “Donation to BDFFP, Manaus, Brazil” in the comments.

Science for Amazon conservation

Since 1979, we have studied how fragmentation of the Amazon rainforest affects biodiversity. We turn science into conservation through long-term research, training new scientists and a continuous presence in the region.
